\# AUROADAS ArchitectureAUROADAS is a modular, ROS2‑based ADAS perception and localization stack designed for real‑time operation in autonomous driving and driver‑assistance systems. The system fuses camera, IMU, GPS, wheel odometry, and optional LiDAR to produce a stable vehicle pose, local map, obstacle detections, and tracked objects.\---\## System OverviewAUROADAS follows a layered architecture:1\. \*\*Sensors Layer\*\*  &#x20;  Provides raw measurements from camera, IMU, GPS, odometry, and LiDAR.2\. \*\*Vision Layer\*\*  &#x20;  Extracts features, tracks them across frames, and produces visual factors.3\. \*\*Fusion Layer\*\*  &#x20;  Multi‑sensor factor‑graph backend integrating IMU, GPS, odometry, and visual constraints.4\. \*\*Mapping Layer\*\*  &#x20;  Builds and maintains a local map, pose graph, and loop closures.5\. \*\*Perception Layer\*\*  &#x20;  Detects obstacles, tracks them, and computes system confidence.6\. \*\*Visualization Layer\*\*  &#x20;  Publishes RViz markers for debugging and evaluation.\---\## High‑Level Pipeline Diagram```mermaidflowchart LR&#x20;
