# AUROADAS Glossary

This glossary defines key terms used in the AUROADAS perception and localization system.

---

## Localization & Mapping

**SLAM** — Simultaneous localization and mapping.  
**VIO** — Visual‑Inertial Odometry.  
**Factor Graph** — Optimization framework combining sensor constraints.  
**IMU Preintegration** — Integrating IMU data between keyframes.  
**Bundle Adjustment** — Joint optimization of poses and 3D points.  
**Loop Closure** — Detecting revisited areas to reduce drift.  
**Pose Graph Optimization** — Optimizing pose constraints.

---

## Multi‑View Geometry

**Essential Matrix** — Encodes relative camera motion.  
**Triangulation** — Recovering 3D points from multiple views.  
**Reprojection Error** — Pixel error between observed and projected point.

---

## Sensor Fusion

**EKF / UKF** — Nonlinear state estimators.  
**Residual** — Difference between predicted and observed measurement.  
**Jacobian** — Partial derivative matrix for optimization.

---

## ADAS Perception

**Object Detection** — Identifying obstacles.  
**Object Tracking** — Estimating object motion.  
**Confidence Score** — Probability of correctness.

---

## Robotics Math

**SE(3)** — 3D rotation + translation.  
**Lie Algebra** — Representation of small motions.

---

## Software & Embedded

**ROS2** — Real‑time robotics middleware.  
**Zero‑Copy Transport** — Passing data without copying.  
**HAL** — Hardware abstraction layer.

---

## Mapping

**Point Cloud** — 3D environment representation.  
**Voxel Grid** — 3D grid representation.  
**Occupancy Grid** — Free/occupied space map.

---

## Safety & Standards

**ISO 26262** — Automotive functional safety standard.  
**ASIL** — Safety integrity classification.

---
