\# AURORA‑ADAS



A modular autonomy architecture for aerial robots integrating:



\- GPU‑accelerated perception

\- Visual‑inertial odometry

\- Factor‑graph SLAM

\- Semantic mapping

\- Hierarchical planning

\- Safety supervision

\- PX4 offboard control



Designed for real‑time deployment on NVIDIA Jetson platforms.
