GAN‑Augmented Kalman Filter Pursuit

Generative Priors for Robust Target Pursuit Under Sensor Degradation

Overview

This project integrates GAN‑generated priors into Kalman filter prediction to stabilize pursuit under noisy or degraded sensor conditions. The approach improves tracking continuity and motion estimation.

Architecture

  • GAN‑based feature refinement
  • Kalman prediction with generative priors
  • Noise‑robust tracking
  • Adaptive pursuit control
