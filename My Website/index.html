About Me

I am an AI/ML Engineer and Robotics Perception Researcher specializing in autonomous systems, multimodal AI, and human-centered robotics. My work spans UAV autonomy in GNSS-denied environments, ADAS perception, and assistive humanoid robots.

I am the creator of MUSVTECH SYSTEMS, a multi-division ecosystem: AUROMUS Autonomy Suite, MUSVTECH Humanoids, and AUROMUSIC AI.
